Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Chesso is a chess learning and hint research project run by Arpan Bhandari and Rijan. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in, our authentication provider Clerk gives us an account ID and the basic profile you share, such as your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Profile settings. Preferences you choose in Chesso, including whether you allow analytics and whether you take part in research.
  • Activity. The puzzles and lessons you work on, the moves you make, hints you view, and timing information.

How we use it

  • To run Chesso: save your progress and show you suitable puzzles.
  • To improve Chesso, if you allow analytics.
  • For research on chess hints, only if you opt in. Research results are published in aggregate and do not identify you.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

We use Clerk (sign-in), Neon (database), Vercel (website hosting), and our own server (the Chesso API). They process data only to provide these services to us.

Your choices

You can change your analytics and research choices, download your records, or delete your Chesso profile at any time from your profile page. You can also contact us to have your data removed.

Contact

Reach us through arpan.sh or rijan.sh.
